Methodology
Hybrid Detection Engine
We analyze transaction networks as graphs, then score every wallet by combining compliance rules with machine learning into a single 0-100 risk score.
Compliance Rules
  • Structuring: below $10K reporting threshold
  • Rapid relay: 8+ hops in under 1 hour
  • Fan-out/fan-in: 1 to 15+ then reconverging
  • Circular flows: bidirectional fund paths
  • Pass-through: forwarding ~100% of inflows
ML Classifier
  • Random Forest on 9 engineered features per wallet
  • Degree, volume, burst score, active hours, ratios
  • Louvain community detection for clustering
  • 8 cluster-level features (density, fan-out, compactness)
  • Balanced class weights for skewed data
risk = min( rule_flags x 12 + ml_probability x 50, 100 )
Rules contribute up to 50 pts. ML contributes up to 50 pts. Combined into one actionable score.
Detection Patterns
Five patterns,
five SAR codes
01
Layering SAR-ML-001
Funds split across intermediaries then merged at destination. Multiple hops to obscure origin.
02
Structuring SAR-ML-002
Transactions kept below $10K CTR threshold. "Smurfing" -- many small transfers replace one large one.
03
Round-Tripping SAR-ML-003
Funds circle back through 5-10 hops. Creates the illusion of legitimate business while money never changes hands.
04
Peel Chains SAR-ML-004
8+ hops in under 1 hour. Each hop peels off a fee while the bulk races forward.
05
Fan-Out / Collection SAR-ML-005
One source to 20+ wallets, then all converge to 1-2 collectors. Common in mixing services.
